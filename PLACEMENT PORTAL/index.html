01 / INTRODUCTION

About the Project

Digital Campus Placement Portal is a menu-driven C++ application designed to simulate the campus recruitment process. It connects students, companies, and placement management through a structured digital workflow.

🎓

Students

Maintain profiles, academic details and job applications.

🏢

Companies

Manage company information and job opportunities.

🏆

Placement

Verify eligibility, rank candidates and track results.

02 / OBJECTIVES

What are we solving?

👨‍🎓

Student Management

Store and manage student details and academic records.

💼

Job Management

Organise companies, job openings and requirements.

✅

Eligibility Verification

Check CGPA, eligible branch and required skills.

📊

Candidate Ranking

Prioritise eligible applicants using a Max-Heap.

04 / OBJECT-ORIENTED DESIGN

Core OOP Concepts

🔒 Encapsulation

Combines data and related functions inside classes.

🎭 Abstraction

The abstract User class defines a common interface.

🌳 Inheritance

Student and PlacementOfficer derive from User.

🔄 Polymorphism

Virtual functions allow derived classes to provide their own behaviour.

User (Abstract Class) → Student | PlacementOfficer

Other important classes: Company, JobOpportunity, Application and PlacementPortal.

07 / DATA STRUCTURE SIMULATION

Max-Heap Ranking

Eligible candidates are prioritised according to their selection scores. A priority queue maintains the highest-priority score at the top.

Selection Score = (CGPA × 10) + Skill Test Marks
